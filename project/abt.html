The project — Ngul Yoen

What it is, why it was built, and what it covers

Ngul Yoen — ngul (money) and yoen (scope) in Dzongkha — is a student project that analyses what drives banking choices in Bhutan. It studies four core factors — trust, service quality, accessibility, and ease of use — across 6 banks and 111 respondents, turning raw survey data into insights and tools that help customers make better financial decisions.

About me

The person behind Ngul Yoen

HL
Hema Luitel 3rd-year · Business Intelligence (Financial Engineering) · Gedu College of Business Studies

During the time of this internship period (Dec 2025 - Jun 2026) I was in my third-year at GCBS, where I studied Bachelors degree in Business Intelligence with major in Financial Engineering — where data analysis, business strategy, and technology meet. This project was my chance to apply that blend to a real-world problem: understanding how and why Bhutanese banking customers choose their banks. This website is my possible solution to banking challenges faced by major Bhutanese banking customers.
