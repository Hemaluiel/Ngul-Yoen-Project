The story behind this platform

Where did all my money go?

A college student's frustration that became a mission.

Hello, I'm Hema Luitel.

Before you explore this website, I want to share the story behind why I created it — because this platform was not just built as a project, but as a solution to a real problem I personally faced.

When I first started college, I struggled to manage my money. No matter how careful I thought I was, my balance would disappear quickly — and I had no clear idea where it was going. Bank mini statements showed too little, and full statements were too complex to understand.

At that moment, I wished my bank provided better tools — something that could help me understand my spending patterns, manage my budget, and make smarter financial decisions.

That experience stayed with me. Later, when I was asked to choose a project, I immediately remembered this struggle. It made me curious — not just about my own situation, but about others:

→ How do other students manage their money?
→ What factors influence people's choice of banks?
→ Are they satisfied with their banking services?
→ And most importantly — can these problems be solved?

I began exploring real customer experiences, analysing banking choices, and identifying common challenges. Through this journey, I realised that many people struggle with the same issues — lack of clarity, poor banking knowledge, and limited tools to make informed decisions.

That's when I decided to build this platform.

This website is designed to act as a personal financial assistant — helping users:

→ Stay updated with banking information
→ Compare banks and choose what suits their needs
→ Analyse their bank statements to understand spending
→ Make better financial decisions with confidence

What started as my personal problem has now become a solution I hope can help many others.
